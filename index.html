Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses, mentorship, and refund process.

How does the 100% refund actually work?

expand_less

You pay the nominal commitment fee (₹699) upon enrollment. Complete 100% of the lessons, submit all assignments scored ≥ 80%, and deploy the required capstone projects within the course challenge period (e.g. 90 days). Once verified, ₹699 is credited to your bank account or UPI ID.

What happens if I miss the challenge deadline?

expand_more

If you miss the deadline, you simply do not receive the cash refund. You do NOT lose course access! You retain lifetime access to continue studying at your own pace and earn your verified certificate whenever you finish.

Do I still retain course access after getting the refund?

expand_more

Yes! Your course access does not expire. You retain full lifetime access to video lectures, code repositories, and future syllabus updates.

How does 1:1 mentorship work?

expand_more

Whenever you encounter an error or logic roadblock, you can request a live 1:1 session from your student portal. Mentors connect in an average of 7 minutes to share screens, debug code, and review project pull requests.

Do I receive an accredited certificate?

expand_more

Yes. Upon completing the curriculum and project milestones, you receive an ISO 9001:2015 accredited digital certificate with a verifiable QR code recognized by over 500+ tech hiring partners across India.
